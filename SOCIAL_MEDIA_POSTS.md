### Option 2: Learning Journey
```
From data cleaning to deployment: Lessons from building an AI system for dementia detection 🧠

After months of research on multimodal MRI analysis, here's what I learned:

1️⃣ Data Quality > Model Complexity
Feature engineering gave us +21% AUC improvement. Architectural changes? Only +<3% AUC. The lesson: invest in understanding your data FIRST.

2️⃣ Honest Evaluation Matters
We separated "honest" features (available at diagnosis) from "circular" features (cognitive tests). The 0.6 → 0.81 AUC jump showed exactly how much biomarkers contribute.

3️⃣ Cross-Dataset Validation is Harsh
Models that worked great on OASIS sometimes collapsed on ADNI. Real-world deployment needs this reality check.

4️⃣ Longitudinal Data Helps (When Done Right)
ResNet features failed (0.52 AUC), but domain-specific biomarker trajectories achieved 0.848 AUC. Feature design matters.

5️⃣ Documentation = Future You's Best Friend
Spent as much time documenting as coding. 2,368 lines of comprehensive docs made the project reproducible and publishable.

📊 Final Results:
• 834 subjects (OASIS + ADNI datasets)
```
